## 🖱️ How to Use AnyDesk

**Connecting to another computer**

Open AnyDesk and type the other person's address into the connection field. Press connect. The other person confirms the request on their screen, and the session begins.

**Letting someone connect to you**

Read your address aloud or send it in a message. When the other person enters it, a prompt appears on your screen asking for permission. Accept it, and they can now view your desktop.

**Moving files**

During a session, use the file transfer option to browse the other computer's folders and copy what you need in either direction.

**Ending a session**

Close the session window or press the disconnect button. The connection drops immediately and the other party loses access.

## 🔐 Security and Privacy

Remote access only works when both sides agree. Nobody can connect to your computer unless you accept the incoming request, and you can end any session instantly.

Sessions are encrypted end to end using TLS. Your screen contents and file transfers are scrambled in transit, which means they cannot be read by anyone intercepting the connection.

You can set a permanent password for unattended access if you want to reach your own computer while away from it, or keep the confirmation prompt enabled for maximum control. Choose whichever fits how you work.
